QuickStudy: flat side-profile owl/page icon concept.
Tool: built-in image_gen. New image, no earlier image references.

Create ONE entirely fresh flat graphic app logo for QuickStudy, a reading and study app. Not an illustration scene. Think carefully designed publisher's emblem or a memorable modern mascot-symbol.

A single compact OWL IN SIDE PROFILE FACING RIGHT. This is a new silhouette, not a front-facing owl mask and not an owl holding anything. One short soft ear tuft, rounded upright head flowing into a compact body, one small triangular apricot beak pointing right. One warm-ivory facial crescent, with ONE modest dark-plum almond eye and a tiny clean ivory catchlight. Calm intent expression, not baby eyes, not angry or smug.

The most important visual idea: the owl's folded WING IS A TURNING BOOK PAGE. One broad warm-ivory wing/page shape sweeps diagonally from the shoulder toward the lower-right, with a single lavender notch indicating a page fold. Its lower edge finishes in a clean curved book-page tip. Anatomical wing and single paper page are one shape, not a separate book or prop. Use thoughtful negative space. Make the whole owl-page silhouette compact, confident and immediately readable. No feet, feather marks, lines of text, extra eyes, separate cards or furniture. No letterforms or wordmark.

Art direction: strong custom 2D VECTOR IDENTITY. Very simple solid filled shapes, clear intentional geometry, beautifully controlled Bezier-like curves and optical balance. 4 colors ONLY: deep muted violet #6650AE for owl body, warm ivory #FFF7EA face/wing, dark plum #30283F eye, apricot #E9AA6A beak. Full-bleed very pale lavender #F6F3FB background. This must feel like a designer drew a distinct logo, not a stock owl graphic.
Zero 3D, zero gradients, zero gloss, zero texture, zero shadows, zero glow, zero bevel. Not a toy, not clay, not isometric, not photorealistic, not a busy emoji icon. Do not reproduce earlier designs.
Composition: square 1:1, centered single emblem occupying about 68% width and 72% height, spacious margins. No outer rounded-square tile, no border. One final icon only, no presentation board, no mockup, no labels, no watermark.
