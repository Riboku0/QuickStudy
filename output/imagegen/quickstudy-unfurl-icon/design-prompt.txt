DESIGN PROMPT
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset: ONE exceptional finished QuickStudy app icon, square, high resolution, for a personal library, reading and flashcard study app.

REFERENCE IMAGE: the attached owl_night.webp contains the established app mascot. Reference ONLY the owl's gentle rounded head, modest ear tufts, paired warm-white face patches, lavender identity, attentive dark-plum eyes and small apricot triangular beak. Ignore its leaves, moon, stars and scenery. You may refine eyes and proportions. Keep recognition as this app's owl rather than replacing it with a generic animal.

ART DIRECTOR'S CONCEPT: THE OWL'S WINGS UNFURL INTO BOOK PAGES.
Invent one beautifully integrated proprietary emblem. At first glance, a distinctive friendly wise owl; on second glance, its two lower wings are the two pages of an open book. The wings themselves ARE pages, not hands holding a separate book. The upper half is a softly rounded owl head, the lower half flows continuously into two broad elegant page-shaped wings that meet at a clean central V-shaped fold. A gentle asymmetric page-turn makes it memorable: viewer-right wing/page curls upward slightly at its outer tip, with ONE offset broad lavender sheet behind it, subtly suggesting a study flashcard. Viewer-left wing/page sweeps down into the fold. This must be a deliberate unified owl/book silhouette, not a collage of a bird, book and cards.
The wing-page shapes have warm-ivory inner surfaces and milky-lavender outer contours; the tiny negative-space book crease defines the fold. No rectangular book cover, no spine, no closed-book body, no wing-fingers. No actual human hands. No bird sitting on a stack of books. No separate held prop. No full bird feet, no tiny feathers. Broad recognizable silhouette with slight gentle asymmetry rather than a heraldic symmetrical owl-mask.

FACE: warm, quietly clever, attentive. Two fully open softly rounded almond-shaped eye areas in a paired warm-ivory face mask. Dark-plum rounded irises of moderate size, a single crisp little ivory catchlight in each, and a very subtle gaze toward the lifted page. No narrowed angry brows, no predatory eye cutouts. Eyes expressive without giant baby-anime circles. No wink, no sleepy eyelids, no lashes, no glasses. One clean small apricot downward beak centered between face patches. The head occupies about 52% of the emblem, so it remains the recognizable established mascot.

COLOR DESIGN: luminous but muted natural harmony, not neon. Full-bleed dark velvety midnight-plum #292337 background, flat and opaque. Main owl lavender #B6A7DE, slightly deeper lavender #8973BE for ONE broad shadow plane, face and inner page wings warm ivory #FFF5E4, irises deep plum #30273F, beak small apricot #F2B879. Let pale lavender and warm ivory dominate the mark, with apricot only as a tiny warm counterpoint. Rich contrast and elegant color balance. This palette belongs to the app's violet-and-pale-surface UI, elevated for a store icon. No electric purple background, no rainbow, teal, charcoal-gray bird, parchment background or metallic gold.

STYLE: sophisticated flat graphic mascot identity with very restrained paper-layer relief, broad matte cel-shaded shapes, pristine smooth vector-like edges, no outlines. Thoughtful visual balance and clean negative space. It should be the work of a strong contemporary identity designer, not a stock 3D toy illustration. No glossy plastic, airbrushed glow, shiny bevels, photorealism, grain, texture, atmospheric lighting, gradients or external drop shadow. No tiny lines or page text. The whole idea should read as an icon at 48 pixels.

COMPOSITION: one large centered emblem, about 76% canvas width and 74% canvas height, generous safe corners. The whole owl-page silhouette is visible; no cropped head or wings. Slightly lively asymmetry is in the lifted page, not in a tilted square tile. Full square opaque background without pre-rounded corners, border or frame. Deliver just ONE finished icon, not a board or mockup.
EXCLUDE: title, text, lettering, numbers, wordmark, logos of other brands, watermark, app UI, scenery, sparkles, stars, moon, hats, badges, decorative circles, flying cards, extra objects.
